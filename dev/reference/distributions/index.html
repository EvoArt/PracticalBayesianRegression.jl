Distribution functions

brms's density (d), distribution (p), quantile (q) and random-draw (r) functions for its families, with brms's argument order and parameterisations. They take scalars; broadcast them with a dot, dskew_normal.(x, 0, 1, 2). Densities take log = false, distribution functions lower_tail = true, log_p = false (R's lower.tail and log.p), and random draws an rng keyword.
